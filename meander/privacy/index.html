Meander — Privacy Policy & Data Deletion

Application: org.isolaforge.meander · Last updated: 18 August 2026

Meander is a walking game developed by Isola Forge. This page explains what data the app stores, how it is used, and how you can have it deleted — including without installing the app.

Who is responsible for this data

Meander is developed by Isola Forge. For any privacy matter, contact isolaforge@gmail.com.

What data we collect

Sign-in happens through Google. The following is stored on our server (Supabase):

  • Your Google account identifier. We never receive or store your password — sign-in is handled entirely by Google.
  • The nickname you chose (visible to other players).
  • Country, inferred from your device settings. This is not precise location.
  • Daily step totals — the number of steps per day, never a route or a location.
  • Your character appearance, avatar frame and avatar background — cosmetic selections visible to other players.
  • Your selected title and name styling (visible to other players).
  • Experience points (XP) and reputation (RP) totals.
  • Featured trophy selection, trophy progress, friend and crew memberships, and milestone echoes sent to friends. Echoes are structured event data — they contain no free text.
  • Crew name, description and welcome message, if you create or manage a crew. These are visible to other players.
  • Safety data: players you have blocked, reports you have submitted about other players, and your setting for who may send you friend requests.

We never collect or store location data. The app records only the number of steps measured by your device's own step sensor.

The following never leave your device and are not sent to our server: height, weight, gender, stride length, and the step counter calibration factor.

How the data is used

Solely to run the game: rankings, friend and crew features, delivering milestone echoes between friends, showing trophy progress to other players, and handling blocks and reports. Other players only ever see your nickname and game statistics — never your email address or any other identifying information.

Third parties

  • Google — sign-in (OAuth). See Google's privacy policy.
  • Supabase — the database and server infrastructure where the data above is stored.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising purposes.

Deleting your account and data

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. There are two ways to remove it.

1. In the app

Open Settings → Account → Delete account. Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

2. By email, without the app

Send a message to isolaforge@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Meander account.

Send it from the same email address you use to sign in to Meander. That address is the only way we can confirm which account is yours. A request sent from a different address cannot be verified and will not be acted upon.

Requests are completed within 30 days, and usually within a few days.

What gets deleted

Everything listed under What data we collect is permanently removed from our server: your account identifier, nickname, country, daily step history, cosmetic selections, XP and reputation, trophies, friendships, crew membership, milestone echoes, and your blocks and reports. If you are the owner of a crew, that crew is disbanded as part of the deletion.

Nothing is retained on our server afterwards. We keep no backup copy of a deleted account.

Game progress stored locally on your phone — such as your resources, crafted items and collected curiosities — is not held on our server and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app evolves. Significant changes will be communicated through the app.
