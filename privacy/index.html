Isola Forge — Privacy Policy

Applies to: isola-forge.pages.dev · Last updated: 5 September 2026

Isola Forge is an independent studio building Android apps and games. This page covers this website and what happens when you contact us.

Our apps have their own separate policies. An app collects different data than a website does, so each one is documented on its own page rather than summarised here:

  • Meander — privacy policy & data deletion

If you are looking for how to delete an account or the data an app holds, that is in the policy for that app, not on this page.

Who is responsible for this data

Isola Forge. For any privacy matter — a question, a request to see your data, or a request to delete it — contact isolaforge@gmail.com. We answer from the same address.

What this website collects

Nothing that we ask for or store ourselves. This site is a set of static pages. It has no analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising, no comment system and no forms — the only way to contact us from it is an ordinary mailto: link that opens your own email app.

It sets no cookies, and it stores nothing in your browser. Because of that there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to.

What the hosting provider sees

The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. Like any web server, it processes the technical details every browser sends when it requests a page — IP address, browser and operating system version, the address requested, the referring page if there is one, and the time of the request. This is used to deliver the page and to protect the service against attacks and abuse. It is not combined with anything else, it is not used to build a profile of you, and we do not receive per-visitor reports from it.

See Cloudflare's privacy policy for how they handle that data.

When you contact us

If you email us, we hold your message and your email address — plus whatever you choose to put in the message. We use it only to answer you and, where a conversation leads to work together, to keep track of that.

Email reaches us through Gmail, so Google processes it in transit and in storage the same way it does any email — Google's privacy policy applies to that part.

We keep correspondence only as long as it is useful for the matter it concerns, and delete it after that. If you would rather we delete an exchange straight away, say so and we will.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data, and we never have.
  • We do not share it for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not track you across other websites.
  • We do not collect location data on this site.

Your rights

If you are in the EU or EEA, the GDPR gives you the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to restrict or object to how it is used, and to receive a copy of it in a portable form. In practice, for this website that means the email correspondence described above.

Send any such request to isolaforge@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days, and usually within a few days.

If you believe we have handled your data improperly, you also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority. In Finland this is the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).

Children

This website is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect anything about them. Where an app of ours has an age rating or its own rules about younger players, those are set out in that app's own policy.

Changes to this policy

This policy is updated when the site or the way we work changes — for example if analytics or a contact form were ever added, which would be stated here before it went live. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
